% Figure 1 (v6): a conventional systems-paper overview, one connected flow from

%     at the group's right edge.
%   Beyond the wall: the passive receiver (vermilion border); (2) a branch of

%   Observer (dashed group, never trained on the target): Enroll -> (3) Match
%     -> (4) Identity on later days; one grey line under it on prior attacks.

\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm, y=1cm, font=\footnotesize]
